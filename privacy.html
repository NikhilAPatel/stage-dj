STAGE

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Stage doesn't collect your data. It has no account, and it doesn't send anything from your device to us or to anyone else.

Your music

The tracks you bring in stay on your device, and nothing's uploaded.

What's kept on your device

Your library, playlists, cue points, settings, recordings and samples are stored in the app, on your device. They go if you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, they may be part of that backup, under your own account's settings.

What you choose to share

You can save a backup of your library, or share a recording, through the system's own sheets. Where those go is up to you, and we don't see them.

Lock Screen and Control Center

The track that's playing shows there, as it does for any music app. That stays on your device.

Crash reports

If you've agreed to share analytics with Apple, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. We use them only to fix the app. You control this in your device's Settings.

Children

The app doesn't collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new policy will be here, with its date, before the app that does it comes out.
