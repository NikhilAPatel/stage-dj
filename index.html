STAGE

Support

Stage is a two-deck DJ app for the music you already own. For help, a bug or an idea, email patel.a.nikhil@gmail.com. It helps to say which phone or iPad you're on, and your version number, which is at the bottom of Settings in the app.

How do I add music?

Open BROWSE and use the import key to bring in audio files from the Files app. Stage plays files you have on your device, and it doesn't play music from streaming services. With no music yet, Try Two Free Tracks gives you something to mix straight away.

How do I start mixing?

In the browser, press 1 or 2 beside a track to put it on that deck, then DECKS to go back, and PLAY. The ? key at the top explains any control you touch without changing the music, and Settings has a guide to all of them.

What if a track's beat or tempo looks wrong?

Touch the BPM on that deck. Its menu lets you fix the beat grid for that track, and what you set is kept.

Will I lose my library if I change phones?

Settings can save a backup of your beat grids, cues, loops, keys and playlists to a file, and restore it later. It doesn't include the music or sampler sounds, so keep those as well.
